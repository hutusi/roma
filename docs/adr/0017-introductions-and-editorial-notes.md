# 0017 — Neutral introductions, with the editorial view in its own short field

Status: accepted (2026-07-26). Reverses the prose half of ADR 0010's editorial workflow and the 口吻 rule the handbook carried from launch; the routing, subset and dual-authoring decisions in ADR 0010 and ADR 0012 are untouched.

## Context

The corpus was AI-drafted off-repo and pasted into `src/db/seed-data/`. ADR 0010 records that the prose was "fact-checked and corrected in both languages" — the facts were checked, the register never was, and it read as machine-written.

Measurement made the problem specific rather than a matter of taste. Across the 74 编辑札记: the superlative 最X的 in 60, the identical five-gram 电影史上最 in 14, an em dash in 50 zh and 70 en, and all 41 创作历程 exactly two paragraphs long in both languages. The strongest signal was not any phrase. zh notes ran 200–240 code points on a 200–500 allowance — a coefficient of variation of 0.044, where every other prose family in the corpus sits at 0.12–0.36. Every note had been written to just clear the publish floor, to one shared arc: premise, technique, historical placement, verdict. That arc was the handbook's own three-question rubric, followed as a checklist.

**Four drafts were attempted before the diagnosis was right**, and the failures are the argument for what follows.

1. *Strip the tells.* Removed the superlatives, closing directives and not-X-but-Y antitheses. Still read stiff — every sentence a balanced pair landing like an aphorism.
2. *Calibrate to the owner's own essays.* Measured ~15k characters of hutusi.com and matched sentence-length dispersion, dash rate and hedging rate. Worse: hitting a hedging target of 2.34 per 1000 characters means inserting 大概 to satisfy a number.
3. *Run the humanizer checklists.* Cut the quotable closers, rule-of-three stacks and negative parallelisms. Still contrived.
4. *Neutral encyclopedic register.* Worked immediately.

The first three failed for one reason: the voice was performed rather than held, and the effort is what shows. Chasing "natural" is self-defeating.

Then the naming problem surfaced, and it explains why those drafts felt wrong even when the sentences were good. 编辑札记 promises a personal voice. Filling that field with encyclopedic prose is a category error, and the reader feels the mismatch before they can name it.

## Decision

- **Split the field.** `introduction` / `introductionEn` on films carries the descriptive body and gates publishing (200–500 code points, 120–350 words). `editorial_note` keeps its column name and gets its meaning back: short, optional, an editor's own voice. People are asymmetric on purpose — `bio` is already a short neutral summary that doubles as the card blurb and meta description, so it *is* 人物介绍 and needed relabelling rather than replacing; people gain only the note columns.
- **Every descriptive field is neutral**: 影片介绍, 人物介绍, 片单引言, 入选理由, and the two long forms, `essay` and `careerEssay`. The standard is that each sentence should be the kind of claim a source could support. The editorial position lives in exactly three places — what is included, the order it is included in, and 编辑札记.
- **The long forms are the body to the introduction's lead.** They were left out of both rule families at first, on the reading that an unheaded long-form field is where a longer argument belongs. That was wrong for the same reason the old 编辑札记 was: on a film page the reader would meet a neutral 影片介绍, a short 编辑札记, and then several paragraphs telling them what to notice, with nothing marking the change of voice. The essay instead carries what will not fit the introduction's 500-code-point band — production history, sourcing, reception, restoration — and repeats none of it.
- **The note is capped, never floored** (400 code points / 250 words). The floor is what did the damage: an editor with one sharp sentence could not satisfy a 200-code-point minimum, so prose was manufactured to fill it. A ceiling cannot be met by writing more. A bound is kept rather than dropped because `essay` is what unbounded already means; without one, the note and the essay become the same field with two names.
- **Notes are optional to publish.** An entity publishes on its introduction alone, so the corpus is never blocked on writing 74 of them, and 收录即推荐 still holds.
- **Delete the three-question rubric** from the handbook outright. It was not wrong about content; it was followed as a checklist and produced the shared arc.
- **A register lint**, `src/db/seed-data/voice.ts`, blocking in CI. Rules declare which families they apply to, which is the load-bearing part: 电影史上最伟大的作品 is a defect in an 影片介绍 and the field working as designed in an 编辑札记.

## Consequences

- **The first lint design was invalid and was rebuilt.** It rewarded length dispersion, because uniformity was the tell in an argumentative register. Encyclopedic prose has a canonical arc, so the rewritten introductions cluster at CV 0.07 — correct here, and that rule would have fired on every good one. What replaces it targets the actual failure mode of a reference register: unfalsifiable evaluation, second-person address, missing identification, and English em dashes. The hutusi.com calibration is retired with it; essays are not the reference genre for a reference field.
- **The superlative rule targets the frame, not the word.** The owner's own prose uses 最X的 at 1.4 per 1000 characters, so banning it outright would be its own artifact. 电影史上最 bets against all of film history; 全片最好的几分钟大概在… is bounded by one film and hedged.
- **Facts became load-bearing, and the error rate is real.** Three of the six pilot introductions had factual errors on first draft — 8½ won two Oscars rather than one, its title arithmetic is contested between sources, and Sunset Boulevard's category is Best Story and Screenplay. The old register concealed this: 怀尔德的刻薄好学 cannot be wrong, only overwrought. The remaining corpus needs a verification pass, not merely a rewrite; the stored `wikidataId`/`imdbId` are the handle.
- **The lint earned itself immediately**, catching what four rounds of human review missed: the English 东京物语 introduction wrote "Yasujiro Ozu" where `directors.ts` has "Yasujirō Ozu", so the page rendered one spelling in the director link and another in the prose below it.
- **The split leaves 68 obsolete notes behind, and clearing them is part of the rollout.** `main` defined `editorialNote` for all 74 films; this ADR's scheme defines 6. `resync-content.ts` deliberately leaves a field seed-data does not define alone rather than nulling it — the ADR 0014 rule that stops a resync clobbering editor-authored work — so a plain `--all --apply` fills every `introduction` *and* keeps the pre-split prose in `editorial_note`, which the new page then renders in its own section. The remedy is `--clear`, generated from the dry run's own output; DEPLOY.md carries the exact commands. Worth stating because the mechanism reads as a safety property right up until the merge, at which point it becomes the defect: the prose this ADR removes would return under a heading claiming it is an editor's voice.
- **Publishing a person now requires 人物介绍.** The gate accepted `bio` *or* 创作历程, which predates this ADR and was defensible while `bio` was just one field among several. It is not, once `bio` **is** 人物介绍: it doubles as the card blurb on every listing and the meta description, so a person published on the essay alone rendered a blank card and empty metadata, and `publishEnProblems` already required `bioEn` — leaving the Chinese gate the laxer of the two.
- **Migration `0014` is purely additive** — four nullable columns, nothing renamed or dropped, safe to run under the live site in DEPLOY.md's order. Backfilling `introduction` from `editorial_note` and clearing the latter happens after the reading code ships. Note that the backfill alone delivers nothing new: it copies whatever the database holds, which is the AI draft, and `resync-content.ts --apply` is what moves rewritten prose out of seed-data.
- **The corpus is through.** All 510 prose units pass: 74 film introductions, 63 person introductions, 41 创作历程, 14 film essays, 6 notes, and 8 lists with their 41 入选理由. The whole-corpus lint assertion was `test.todo` while the rewrite was in flight — a suite that fails on known-pending work teaches people to ignore it — and is live now, so new prose that trips a rule fails CI.
- **`verify-facts.ts` reports ten runtime disagreements with Wikidata, and none of them is a defect.** A film has one runtime per cut, and the two sources are often counting different ones: 205 against 185 for Andrei Rublev is the original cut against the released one. It is printed as a note rather than a mismatch for exactly this reason. Mechanical year and director checks pass on all 74.
- **Deferred: `seed_prose_baseline`.** ADR 0014's three-way-merge remedy generalizes to prose, and would make routine resyncs safe without a manual audit. Not built — it needs a migration, a bootstrap story with the same one-time blind spot, and its own tests, and a one-shot rewrite is served by auditing once from `main` instead.

## Not changed

ADR 0010's dual-authoring and en-subset rules — both editions are still authored, never translated, and matching sentence counts across them remain a defect signal. ADR 0012's routing and en-pending stubs. ADR 0014's tag baseline and the prose-only scope of `resync-content.ts`. 顺序即立场: the order of a 片单 is still an editorial statement, and is now one of the few places one is made.
